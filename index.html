Assistant Professor of Marketing

Cornell Tech and SC Johnson College of Business

I study digital marketing, mobile advertising, personalization, and privacy through two complementary lenses: how recent advances in machine learning can create value in digital marketplaces, and how theory-driven structural frameworks reveal the marketing and economic implications of those advances.
